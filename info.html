1. Loading Resources

Paste a YouTube link into the resource bar on the main page and press Load to open the embedded player.

2. Panic Screen

Press the Esc (Escape) key at any time to quickly switch to a Google Classroom-style panic screen. This stops playback immediately.

3. Returning Home

Type back (without quotes) to return to the dashboard, or refresh the page to reset everything.
